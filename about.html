About

Meet Malika Shaw

Malika Shaw is the founder of Anytime Anywhere Mobile Notary Services LLC, serving individuals, families, professionals, and organizations throughout Metro Atlanta. Malika provides dependable mobile appointments with an emphasis on professionalism, privacy, accuracy, clear communication, and respectful customer service.

Whether a client needs an appointment at home, at an office, in a hospital, or at a senior-care facility, Malika works to make the notarization process as convenient and organized as possible.

Her goal is simple: arrive prepared, communicate clearly, protect the integrity of the notarization process, and provide a professional experience from the first call through completion.

Malika also holds a Bachelor of Science in Accounting, bringing a strong background in professionalism, document organization, accuracy, and confidentiality to every appointment.
