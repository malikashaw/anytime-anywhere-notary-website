Possible Additional Charges

Depending on the appointment, some requests involve additional charges. Anything that applies to your appointment is disclosed and approved before service.

  • Additional travel beyond the standard service area
  • Extended appointment or waiting time
  • Parking and tolls
  • Printing and scanbacks
  • Courier or shipping
  • Witness coordination
  • Same-day or urgent service
  • Evening, weekend, or holiday service
  • Additional signers or locations
All applicable notarial, travel, convenience, printing, parking, courier, witness, after-hours, and facilitation charges will be disclosed before service. Submitting an appointment request does not confirm an appointment or a final price.
