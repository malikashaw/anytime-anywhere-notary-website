Our Services

Mobile Notary & Signing Services

We travel to you across Metro Atlanta. Each request is reviewed individually and remains subject to Georgia law and standard notarial requirements. We are not attorneys and cannot select forms, prepare legal documents, or provide legal advice.

General Mobile Notary

Acknowledgments, jurats, affidavits, authorization forms, school and travel documents, business paperwork, and other eligible documents, notarized at a location that works for you.

Hospital & Bedside Notary

Mobile appointments at hospitals, rehabilitation centers, hospice locations, and other care settings, handled with patience and discretion.

Nursing Home & Assisted-Living Appointments

On-site notarizations for residents of nursing homes and assisted-living communities, coordinated around visiting hours and facility procedures.

Estate Planning Document Signings

Notarization support for powers of attorney, advance directives, trusts, and related documents that you or your attorney have already prepared.

Documents must be prepared by you or your attorney. We do not draft documents or give legal advice.

Apostille Facilitation

Coordination, review, submission, and courier assistance for documents intended for international use.

Facilitation and courier assistance only. Apostille issuance and acceptance are decided by the appropriate government authority.

I-9 Authorized Representative Services

In-person document inspection and Section 2 completion support when an employer designates you as its authorized representative.

The employer must designate the authorized representative. This service is separate from notarization unless a separate notarial act is required.
