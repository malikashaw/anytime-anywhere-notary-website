Privacy Policy

This is a general starting template. Please review it and adjust it to reflect your actual practices before publishing. It is not legal advice.

Anytime Anywhere Mobile Notary Services LLC ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information you share when you contact us or request an appointment.

Information We Collect

When you contact us or submit an appointment request, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, appointment location or ZIP code, and details about the service you are requesting. We ask that you do not submit Social Security numbers, account numbers, identification images, medical records, or other confidential documents through our website form.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your request, schedule and confirm appointments, disclose applicable fees, and provide our services. We do not sell your personal information.

How Your Information Is Shared

We share information only as needed to provide our services, such as with a secure form or scheduling provider, or when required by law. Appointment requests submitted through our website are handled by a third-party secure form provider; that provider's own privacy practices also apply.

Data Retention and Security

We keep appointment-related information only as long as reasonably necessary and take reasonable steps to protect it. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Text Messaging

If you contact us by text or consent to text contact, we may communicate with you by text about your appointment. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out at any time by replying to let us know.

Your Choices

You may request that we update or delete the information you have shared with us by contacting us using the details below.
